Australian Urban Wildlife

Wild Cities

Who still lives among us?

Goal: Investigate wildlife living in Australian urban environments. Compare observations of birds, mammals, reptiles and insects across cities to explore how biodiversity changes in urban areas.

Target audience: Australian urban planners and local policymakers interested in using biodiversity evidence to inform green-space planning, habitat protection and urban conservation.

Wildlife across Australia's cities

Recorded species richness varies considerably across the eight urban areas in this study. The composition of that richness also differs between birds, mammals, reptiles and insects.
